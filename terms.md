Last updated: June 2026

These Terms of Service apply to the Earn2Trade Discord bot and related Discord community features.

The Earn2Trade Discord bot is used to support Earn2Trade community operations inside Discord. Its features may include community events, giveaways, engagement tracking, moderation helpers, trading education activities, game/activity launches, role-based access checks, and administrative tools.

By interacting with the Earn2Trade Discord bot, you agree to use it only for lawful, respectful, and community-appropriate purposes. You must also follow Discord’s Terms of Service, Discord’s Community Guidelines, and the rules of the Earn2Trade Discord server.

You may not use the bot to abuse, exploit, spam, harass, manipulate giveaways, bypass restrictions, or interfere with the normal operation of the community.

## 3. Community Rewards, Giveaways, and Events

The bot may help manage community rewards, event participation, giveaway entries, winner selection, engagement points, or similar community systems.

Participation in events or giveaways does not guarantee a reward. Earn2Trade may review, adjust, reject, or revoke rewards, entries, or eligibility where abuse, error, duplicate participation, suspicious activity, or rule violations are detected.

Earn2Trade staff decisions regarding community events, rewards, and giveaways are final.

## 4. No Trading or Financial Advice

The bot may be used in a trading education community, but the bot itself does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Any information, game, quiz, activity, or community interaction provided through the bot is for educational or entertainment purposes only.

## 5. Availability and Changes

Earn2Trade may update, disable, remove, or modify bot features at any time. The bot may occasionally be unavailable due to maintenance, Discord outages, technical issues, or other reasons.

## 6. Enforcement

Earn2Trade may restrict or remove access to bot features if a user violates these terms, Discord rules, or Earn2Trade community rules.

## 7. Contact

For questions about the Earn2Trade Discord bot, contact Earn2Trade through the official Earn2Trade support channels or website.
