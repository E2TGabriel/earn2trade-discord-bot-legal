# Earn2Trade Discord Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Earn2Trade Discord bot processes information when used inside the Earn2Trade Discord community.

## 1. Information We Process

The bot may process Discord-related information needed to provide community features, including:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server IDs
* Discord channel IDs
* Discord role IDs
* Message metadata such as timestamps and channel context
* Event participation records
* Giveaway entries and winner records
* Engagement or activity counters
* Bot command usage
* Moderation or administrative action records

Some bot features may require access to message content in designated Discord channels, such as legacy prefix commands, message-based event participation, engagement tracking, moderation helpers, or community automation.

## 2. Why We Process This Information

We process this information to operate Earn2Trade Discord community features, including:

* Running events, games, giveaways, and community activities
* Tracking participation and engagement
* Managing eligibility for rewards or giveaways
* Supporting role-based access and permissions
* Helping staff moderate and administer the community
* Preventing abuse, duplicate entries, spam, or manipulation
* Troubleshooting and maintaining bot functionality

## 3. Message Content

The bot may read message content where required for specific Discord features, such as prefix commands, chat-based participation, moderation helpers, or engagement/event tracking.

The bot does not use message content for advertising, resale, unrelated profiling, or external marketing. Where possible, the bot stores only the minimum information needed for the relevant feature.

## 4. Data Sharing

Bot data is used for Earn2Trade community operations. We do not sell Discord user data.

Information may be visible to Earn2Trade staff where needed to operate events, review rewards, moderate the community, investigate issues, or maintain the bot.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep bot-related records only as long as needed for community operations, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, moderation history, event history, reward tracking, or technical maintenance.

## 6. User Requests

Users may contact Earn2Trade through official support channels to ask questions about bot-related data or request assistance.

Some records may need to be retained where required for security, moderation, abuse prevention, reward history, or operational integrity.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect bot-related data and limit access to people who need it for community operations, support, moderation, or maintenance.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when bot features, community operations, or legal requirements change.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions about the Earn2Trade Discord bot, contact Earn2Trade through the official Earn2Trade support channels or website.
